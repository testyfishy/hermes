Privacy policy

Hermes · last updated 8 November 2026

Who uses Hermes

Hermes is a self-hosted personal assistant. Its only user is its owner, who connects their own Google and Microsoft accounts. It is not available to anyone else.

What data Hermes accesses

With the owner's permission, Hermes accesses:

  • Gmail: reading messages, applying labels, archiving, and creating drafts. Hermes has no function to send email.
  • Google Calendar: reading and editing events and calendars.
  • Google Tasks: reading and editing task lists.
  • Basic profile: the account's email address, to tell accounts apart.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide features to the owner: summarising email, drafting replies, keeping the calendar and tasks up to date, and producing a daily summary. It is processed on computers the owner controls, by a language model running on those computers.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and working notes are stored only on the owner's own computers.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train general AI or machine-learning models.
  • No analytics or tracking is used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Hermes's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or account.live.com/consent/Manage (Microsoft), and delete the locally stored tokens.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/testyfishy/hermes.
