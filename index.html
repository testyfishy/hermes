Hermes

A private, self-hosted personal assistant.

Hermes is a personal assistant that one person runs on their own computers, for their own accounts only. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Reads and organises the owner's email, and writes draft replies for the owner to review and send. Hermes cannot send email.
  • Reads and updates the owner's calendar and task lists.
  • Prepares a short daily summary of the owner's schedule and open tasks.

How it handles data

Everything runs on hardware the owner controls, using a language model that also runs locally. Account data is not sent to third-party AI services, sold, or shared. Details are in the privacy policy.
